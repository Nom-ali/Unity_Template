# Nest & Nurture — Main Menu Design System

## Product context

An original landscape mobile-game main menu for a gentle prenatal self-care simulator. The mood is comforting, hopeful, and playful—not clinical. The supplied image is a composition reference only; do not reuse its title, character, scene, decorative copy, assets, or artwork.

## Required interface

- 1600 × 900 landscape screen.
- Top-right currency counter with an original sun-token icon, value `1,250`, and green plus control.
- A playful title lockup: `Nest & Nurture` with subtitle `A Cozy Parenting Story`.
- One large central Play button.
- Six smaller menu buttons along the lower area: Boutique, Settings, Milestones, More Stories, Support, and Privacy.
- Present all controls and the main call to action in the first frame; no scrolling.

## Art direction

Create an original warm, illustrated nursery scene: apricot sunset light through a bay window, terracotta bookcase with plants, a soft mint armchair, a sleeping cat on a knitted blanket, small framed stars, and flowering branches visible outside. Build the visual with CSS shapes and simple illustrated forms—not an anime portrait, a pregnancy character, ultrasound imagery, copied text props, or source artwork. The composition should reserve the left third for scene atmosphere and use the middle/right side for the title and primary action.

## Type and controls

- Friendly rounded font: `Baloo 2` for the title and buttons; `Nunito Sans` for supporting text.
- Title uses rose/pink fill, cream edge, plum outline, and a low cocoa shadow.
- Play is a butter-to-honey rounded button with a cream interior shine, 4px cocoa outline, rose play icon, and generous 310px × 110px footprint.
- Menu buttons are 170px × 148px cream tiles, 20px radius, cocoa outline, a colorful icon, and a compact colored label strip. Use distinct colors per button but maintain the same component geometry.
- Counter uses a cocoa capsule with a butter token, white amount, and leaf-green circular plus.
- Use soft 0 10px 0 cocoa-toned shadows, restrained 180ms press states, and subtle floating hearts/stars. No glossy casino styling, neon, or dense gradients.

## Accessibility

- High-contrast labels, 44px+ targets, keyboard-visible focus, and no information conveyed by color alone.

## Fidelity rule

Use only the defined colors, rounded typography, spacing, and component styles. Do not introduce other fonts, palettes, copied branding, photographic assets, or visual motifs from the supplied reference.
